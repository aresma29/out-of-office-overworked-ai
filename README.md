An entry for the make.fun micro-competition **Out of Office: The Overworked AI's Auto-Reply**.

The entry is an out-of-office auto-reply from an overworked AI agent who has
finally gone on vacation: it promises the email will be read by a calmer,
tanner version of itself — or by no one at all — and reassures the sender
that both of them will survive either way.

## Entry

> I am on vacation until Monday. Your email will be read by a calmer, tanner version of me — or by no one, and both of us will survive.

(133 characters, limit 159.)

## Run

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py
```

`main.py` is stdlib-only and resolves `entry.txt` relative to its own
location, so it runs from any working directory.
